Amazon Trend Pins

About the Application

Amazon Trend Pins is a personal content-management and publishing application designed to help discover relevant seasonal product ideas, prepare Pinterest content, and publish selected product Pins to the user's Pinterest account through the official Pinterest API.

Purpose

The application is used to organize product research and create product-related Pinterest content for an affiliate marketing workflow.
