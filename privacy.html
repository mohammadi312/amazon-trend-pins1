Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Amazon Trend Pins is a personal content-management and publishing application used to create and manage Pinterest content.

2. Information Accessed

When authorized by the account owner, the application may access information provided through the Pinterest API that is necessary to authenticate the account, access relevant boards, and create or manage Pins.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used only to provide the application's intended functionality, including creating and managing Pinterest content.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information. Information accessed through Pinterest is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested functionality or where required by law.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and information accessed through the application.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality.

7. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with this project.
